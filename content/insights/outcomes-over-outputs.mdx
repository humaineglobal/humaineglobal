# Outcomes Over Outputs — A Better Measure of AI Value

The work is not finished when a model ships. It is finished when a meaningful business outcome changes.

The most useful metrics connect adoption and experience to lower cost, faster decisions, retention, revenue, and a durable capability inside the organization.
