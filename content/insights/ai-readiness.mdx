# The Executive Guide to AI Readiness

A practical framework for leaders who need a clear path from AI ambition to measurable enterprise value.

Readiness starts with opportunity, not models. Evaluate customer and business use cases, data maturity, operating ownership, risk controls, and the mechanism that turns a prototype into value.
